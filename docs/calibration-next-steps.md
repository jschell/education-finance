## Ordered follow-up

1. **Diagnostic audit, without changing forecasts.** Decompose existing paired tuition/housing errors by sector, origin, known/withheld identity and horizon; retain year clustering. Inspect signed errors as well as absolute errors and interval width. Audit supplied IPEDS imputation flags against the year-specific dictionary before interpreting their codes. Check missing and changed-sector cases rather than dropping them. Publish how sensitive conclusions are to clearly identified source-quality groups.
2. **Freeze one revised candidate and its test rule before running it.** The existing hierarchy and error-curve findings are consumed evidence. Specify a single coherent process for shared real-cost growth, persistent school variation and innovations, with uncertainty sources and cross-student dependence explicit. Use origin data only. Do not add multiple correction/spread layers to chase the final results. Define supported horizons, numerical tolerances, and sector/withheld-school protections in the protocol; retain failed results.
3. **Known-process verification.** Before historical scoring, verify recovery of deterministic growth, stationary school heterogeneity, changing sector trends and paired shocks. Check that school effects persist, shared identities share them, different schools retain common macro shocks, and parameter/process variation is not counted twice.
4. **Cost and funding evaluation.** Reuse proper origin-budget normalization and matched cases, with separate known/withheld and horizon qualification. Run the candidate through actual bill ledgers and dated individual/capped household policies. Report profile-specific reliability and earlier-completed baseline skill, not just aggregate cost scores. Any broadened identity or threshold study needs its own prespecified selection rule and support counts.
5. **Promotion review, then delivery.** Cost qualification is necessary evidence, not sufficient for forward funding claims. Keep unsupported horizons explicit. Require funding and implementation checks before activating anything; update the model version only for actual model behavior changes. If qualification fails, publish the failed comparison and keep production unchanged.

Historical data expansion and source refresh can proceed alongside diagnosis once their source/definition contract is written. Original publication vintages, population changes and the FIZ/CIZ market-method transition need explicit treatment. No live school scraping, private family data or recurring task is introduced by this plan.

## Completion criteria

It may finish with non-promotion. Stronger long-horizon calibration requires actual additional comparable outcomes; that evidence cannot be created by software tests.
