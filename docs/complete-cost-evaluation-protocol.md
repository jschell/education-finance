## 3. Fixed decision criteria and stopping rule

An economic candidate can proceed only if **every** numerical/source/coverage gate is supported. Main-cell outer availability and complete-bootstrap availability must each be >=90% at every actual depth. In each depth/wait, 80% bill-band coverage must lie in [70%,90%] and its 95% Wilson interval contain .8. Finite feasible 90th-percentile required-deposit coverage must be >=80%; report impossible bounds separately, never count infinity<=infinity as a feasible recommendation. Across at least 32 matched outer worlds per depth/wait, bootstrap interval score must improve >=5% relative to old REML plug-in, neither base individual nor household Brier may worsen by >.02, and at least one must improve by >=.01. All paired effects and sampling uncertainty are reported; no selection from stress/control cells.

For main cells, fitted covariance variance ratios' medians for every family/component must stay within [.5,2] and median drift bias within ±.01. Numerical retained-case portability must hold under the declared tolerances. These are broad finite-sample qualification gates, not guarantees of future financial outcomes. Missing matched support is **unsupported**, not a pass. If any gate fails, reject this candidate for promotion and conclude the evaluation; do not add another correction, change seeds or extend the experiment to seek a pass.
