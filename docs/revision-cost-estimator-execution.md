## Matched graph adapter

Verify the canonical panel identity, requested pinned hash, raw dictionary identities and compressed shard hashes before indexing. Exclude collections at/after origin before any eligibility, cohort, common-pool or donor work. Use the existing pure price/definition/population checks. For each institutional paired tuition/housing transition, require original-current-year and same-file endpoints and their same-year representation matches. Keep reasons for every missing/incomparable pair. Require matching directory name/control/level/sector across selected endpoints; a name difference is a conservative identity exclusion, not proof of a new institution.

To avoid implicitly bridging definition breaks or missing years, use each institution's longest contiguous matched paired segment with at least three transitions; ties prefer the latest segment. This is origin-only support selection, independent of economic values except positivity. Match segments identically across policies; report discarded support. Select at most 200 non-withheld institutions by existing identity hash; up to six known and six withheld are named separately. Withheld histories must never enter either fitting graph. Retain separate unmatched/withheld support counts rather than populating fitting records with evaluation identities.

Each fitting graph stores unique paired source representations, exact component keys/flags/definitions/directory provenance, August CPI deflators and paired real log levels. A coherent edge retains its two observations from one collection. Stitched edges retain the two original-current-year observations. Deduplicate identical endpoint keys across edges; conflicting duplicate payloads fail. Deduplication is an edge-to-observation normalization, not permission for duplicate raw observations. Revisions never add economic years. Original files and all earlier diagnostics remain unchanged.

## Numerical likelihood and bounded optimization

Eliminate each school's unknown paired initial offset with contrasts to its first observation. This is the diffuse-offset likelihood of contrasts (a parameter-independent contrast transform), not a regression that mistakes a reporting revision for growth. Covariance uses exact academic times and source keys: shared cumulative shocks, school cumulative shocks, stationary AR(0.5) deviations and independent source errors. Paired covariance is retained for all four families. A source representation appearing on two edges is a single noisy observation.

Compute school block factorizations plus a low-rank shared-year Woodbury solve. Verify against an independently assembled dense covariance/likelihood on small graphs, including cross-school and same-year cross-source covariance. Never add an undisclosed diagonal jitter to rescue singular fits. Profile the two drift coefficients analytically under [-0.10,0.20], including correlated active-bound solutions. This makes drift start 0 versus 0.03 redundant; record the six prescribed starts (three common standard-deviation starts times two drift starts), explicitly recording reused covariance optimizations rather than inventing additional independent fits. The all-zero covariance start is singular and must be retained as unavailable.

Use pinned research-only NumPy 2.3.5 / SciPy 1.17.0 for Cholesky solves and bounded L-BFGS-B. This is a concrete numerical research requirement, not a new browser/application dependency. Implement analytic covariance derivatives, checked against finite differences. Four families each have two standard deviations [0,0.50] and correlation [-0.95,0.95]. The objective is the same joint training likelihood from the frozen protocol. Use fixed finite starts 0.02 and 0.10, correlations zero, and at most 2,000 iterations per start. Record every start and optimizer exit. Convergence requires both relative objective change and maximum parameter change below 1e-8; a generic optimizer success flag alone is insufficient. Failed or boundary solutions remain visible.

Analytic covariance-family design rank/condition (column scaled, condition >1e8 unsupported), fewer than three academic transitions, finite-difference likelihood curvature, and directional likelihood profiles are separate diagnostics. Directional profiles are not nuisance-reoptimized confidence intervals. A flat curvature/profile or missing repeated-source support marks the decomposition unsupported even if numerical optimization converges. No confidence interval or historical identification claim follows from local curvature. Exact nonidentifiability (one institution, indistinguishable shared/school innovations) must fail explicitly.

## Experiments fixed before execution

- Independent dense-versus-block likelihood, paired covariance, analytic-gradient, offset-invariance, source-key, duplicate, origin, sector/name/basis, withheld-pool and missing-support tests.
- Ideal finite-sample recovery: six fixed seeds 2026111801–2026111806; eight synthetic schools, 24 academic years, two distinct collections per academic year; stationary temporary states. Paired drift (0.02,0.01). Family standard deviations/correlation: shared (0.03,0.02,0.4), permanent (0.04,0.03,0.2), temporary innovations (0.05,0.04,0.3), source errors (0.02,0.03,0.1). Compare estimated paired drifts and covariance diagonals to known truth without tuning against historical results.
- Recovery gate: every replicate has a numerically converged selected start and supported design; across replicates median absolute drift error <=0.03 for both components and each family's/component's median estimated variance/truth ratio in [0.5,2]. Also publish individual failures, boundary hits, condition/profile flags and correlation estimates. These broad mechanism thresholds do not establish economic coverage or remove temporal dependence. Any failed gate keeps historical fitting blocked; do not change seeds, bounds, tolerances or model to rescue it in this stage.
- Misspecification: reuse the same six seed worlds with an additional fixed paired systematic reporting shift (0.10,0.08) from academic year 12 onward shared across source representations. Compare inferred drift/variance changes and retain the already demonstrated economic/reporting observational equivalence. This is a failure-mode sensitivity, not another candidate or a calibrated reporting-bias model.
- Unsupported graph: a single institution and a short temporal graph must return explicit identification/support failure. Exhausted optimization must return nonconvergence; never substitute algebraic moments as the fitted state-space estimator.

Publish reproducible source-identified results with supplied truth, every replicate/start status and the recovery gate. Numerical tolerances/rounding must be explicit; bitwise equality of floating-point optimizer results across BLAS/platforms is not assumed. Offline CI verifies source integrity and numerical reproduction within declared tolerance. No actual school price likelihood or economic score is computed until this pre-fit stage is reviewed and its gate passes.
