Base: main `61adc1dedd7271db3cc230e0b44b4a845c970534`. Implement app 0.15 with additive methodology `funding-decisions-1.0.0`; the model12 economic process, saved schema5 funding, active foundation and all archived evidence remain unchanged.

## Deliverables and order

1. Add a pure, ephemeral decision registry: one extra attendance year, six/twelve months of interrupted pre-entry deposits, grants ending after the first attendance year, lost first-year income and gifts delayed six months. Report inapplicable cases explicitly. Use matched paths and retain the extra year's full bills; never extend funding or grants automatically. Publish sample frequency, median/tail unpaid bills and dated example first shortfall. Alternatives have no event probabilities.
2. Compare entered proportional deposits with a dated-bill-priority policy under exactly the same monthly household budget, eligible deposit dates and earmarked opening funds. Work in cents, preserve every month's available deposits and apply returns, gifts, deposits, then bills. Priority may use origin-reference future bills and current realized balances/bills, never future sampled returns or prices. No transfers, savings optimization or universal dominance claim.
3. Add a separately identified decision-boundary laboratory. Freeze selection at origin: synthetic cash/balanced, four-year bills, waits 0/4/8, opening resources at 95/100/105% of the simulated 80/90/95% required-opening quantiles. Fit and score independent draws; deduplicate equal resource levels and retain labels. Compare stochastic probabilities with a fixed-reference ledger on identical cases. Register actually binding sibling caps, keep deposit windows open through attendance, and compare policies on paired worlds. These are controlled accounting/model tests, not observed economic calibration. Historical origin-only evaluation remains a separate next deliverable; do not relabel synthetic outcomes as historical observations.
4. Integrate collapsed diagnostic tables into the existing Calculate output and JSON export. Do not change saved inputs or the main gauge. Run financial/reproducibility checks and project-prefix phone/iPad/keyboard browser acceptance. Record exact branch/commit/CI/deployment state in continuity.

## Acceptance and limits

Tests must cover an extra-year $25,000 gap, a twelve-month $6,000 deposit interruption, aid/income/gift timing, delayed gifts beyond the goal, cap conservation to cents, ownership, policy nonanticipativity and a strictly improved feasible sibling counterexample. Both methods use the same named worlds. Boundary selection uses fitting draws only; score draws cannot influence selection or refitting. Report interior forecast support, failures and degenerate thresholds, not just aggregate scores.

Runtime diagnostics use at most 256 paired draws and are descriptive; the independently verified savings-target solver is unchanged. Use central economic assumptions and label the scope. Existing historical reports, promotion gates and prospective snapshots remain immutable. The bounded 30-event price trace and a new cost candidate are independent unfinished research; this release cannot resolve future forecast calibration.
