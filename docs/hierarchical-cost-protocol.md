# Coherent school-cost candidate protocol

Frozen before candidate evaluation. This is a candidate; activation requires the existing overhaul eligible-horizon rule. No retrospective adjustment may erase a failed score or create missing support.

Fit on positive consecutive tuition/housing pairs through each origin only. Current fixed-school panels carry survivor selection; report it. Use August CPI endpoints. Model real paired log growth as sector mean + annual common shock + persistent institution effect + institution-year innovation. Estimate sector means over all available training years. Center paired common and individual innovations. Estimate school-effect variance after subtracting within-school sampling variance; shrink known-school means toward the sector. Unknown-school effects are persistent paired draws with deconvolved variance. Sector-mean uncertainty is a persistent paired common-shock draw divided by sqrt(distinct training transitions). This is a finite-sample approximation, not a posterior coverage guarantee.

Use one paired common innovation per economic year across students and one persistent/annual draw per exact school key. Exact UNITID or curated school key shares latent effects; unspecified schools use explicit independent keys. User-entered costs anchor price levels. Apply CPI once. Do not stack legacy cost corrections, trend-window deviations, uncertainty spreads or residual curves on the candidate.

Compare the corrected foundation against the complete candidate using the same synthetic production ledger, current fixed-school sector price levels, origins 2011–2020, waits 0/4/8, and four attendance years. Use 400 matched paths per origin/sector/wait and a fixed seed (850213). Score normalized 80% interval scores, coverage, bias and miss direction. Each candidate is prespecified; no curve selection or residual refitting. Evaluate only completed earlier outcomes for gate eligibility. Require at least three eligible origins for every requested horizon and a 5% score improvement at each supported horizon, with closer 80% coverage and bias protection. Missing support is insufficient evidence and prevents promotion. Additional per-school descriptive growth evidence does not create independent macro histories.

The legacy tuition extension remains a separately identified research bridge until student-population/field continuity passes audit. It cannot silently add housing or whole-plan outcomes. No current SPF anchors enter historical fits. Retain old reports and the original prospective snapshot unchanged.
