# Dated documentary tuition evidence — bounded contract

2026-11-18 America/Los_Angeles. Method `tuition-documentary-1.0.0`.
Base accepted source implementation `88b93bf99671bd010d2ff786ba10e2bbc578ca93`.
Publish this contract before implementation, systematic chart transcription or
new eligibility decisions. Two UAB 2006-report chart labels and the current NCES
preservation-policy lead were already explored during the preceding acquisition;
these are disclosed development evidence. This is not an untouched model trial.

## Fixed sources and measure

Use only the four already pinned NCES Data Feedback Reports, 2005 and 2006 for
UNITIDs 100654 and 100663. No new downloads, institution expansion or predictor.
Read Figure 3's institution series for all three labeled academic years in each
report: at most twelve slots. Retain every expected year even if its point label
is obscured. A figure-comparison-group median is not the institution's charge.

The measure is annual published tuition plus required fees for the report's
full-time first-time degree/certificate-seeking undergraduate cohort, using its
**lowest reported residency category**. Do not relabel this as in-state tuition,
net price, housing or a complete paired bill. Transcribe visible numeric labels
only. Where overlapping marks or labels prevent an independent reading, mark
unreadable with reason; never infer a label from the panel, another report,
neighboring point or expected result. Pin exact document hash, physical PDF page,
figure, year, series identity and two-person transcription review.

## Dates, preservation and permissible claims

Record each report's printed issue date as a documentary date claim, citing its
letter page. An issue-date label is not cryptographic evidence that today's PDF
bytes were downloadable on that date. Current indexed NCES guidance says DFRs
are not regenerated with later final/revised data; the raw policy HTML was not
captured and its redirect-loop failure is retained. This guidance can motivate
a source-specific preservation lead, but cannot certify original raw CSV bytes,
all institution fields, campus population or historical publication availability.
No retry or substitute URL is part of this pass.

Each readable/reviewed slot may qualify only as a **date-claimed documentary
fact** under this new narrow contract. Original byte-vintage, original-price-plus-
directory eligibility, source-population bridging, paired history and production
qualification remain unsupported/false. A claim must retain that proof level.
The validator is a consistency check of pinned documents and independent review,
not an external authenticity oracle.

## Bounded comparisons and validation

For each institution, compare readable slots for overlapping academic years
across its two reports under the same printed measure. Record equal/different/
unavailable states and actual denominators; identical labels cannot establish
unchanged campus aggregation. Do not choose a preferred value after comparison.
The original panel remains unchanged. No model fit, forecasting, random seed,
coverage gate, source bridge, historical backtest or production activation runs.

The separate registry and validator must reject unpinned/changed document hashes,
unknown reports/years, missing or duplicate expected slots, comparison-series
substitution, unreadable numeric values, absent independent reviewers, an
unbound issue-date claim and any stronger proof label/activation request. A
malformed record must not be counted as documentary-eligible. Recompute all
cross-report comparisons from the retained slots; altered outcomes fail.

## Deliverable and stopping rule

Deliver a fixed twelve-slot corpus (or explicit source-caption mismatch if a
report actually has a different declared year range), report/page/date/measure
provenance, independent chart review, deterministic overlap comparisons and
meaningful validator tests. Preserve unreadable cells. Reuse the prior accepted
PDF bytes. Add a fast dedicated workflow, run relevant tests and npm run check,
and publish the result with exact commits/CI. Stop after these four reports.
If no stronger original-publication proof exists, publish that limit rather than
extending scope or promoting documentary evidence into a historical backtest.
