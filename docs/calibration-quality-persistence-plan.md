This is descriptive post-results diagnosis, not an untouched economic experiment or a production model change. Existing final periods are consumed evidence. ## Work to complete

1. Retrieve official NCES year-specific academic-year price dictionaries for 2000–2023. Pin original dictionary bytes, URLs, member names and SHA-256; extract only needed field/flag definitions and their code labels. Verify each year separately; unresolved years/codes remain unclassified. Do not infer code meanings from another year or interpret analyst corrections as independent quotes. Keep the original selected data unchanged.
2. Reconcile every registered baseline bill case with its raw source rows and dictionary. Report observed/missing/unreached/changed-sector counts and price-flag groups over the complete origin-to-last-bill interval. Compare all cases against strict reported-only and reported-or-analyst-corrected subsets where definitions support that distinction. Preserve unavailable counts and report changes in start years/sectors/identities. This is fixed-forecast sensitivity, not refitting on outcomes or a new candidate gate.
3. Diagnose annual real-growth persistence in a fixed origin-defined **2010** cohort selected by the existing cohort rule (stable positive prices 2000–2010). Reconstruct consecutive real tuition/housing log growth; use known/training identities only to calculate each sector/year common mean. Retain withheld identities solely as diagnostics. Later absences, sector changes and missing prices remain explicit attrition; no future-survivor selection or zero fill.
4. Compare nonoverlapping transition windows **2000–2004 vs 2005–2009**, **2000–2009 vs 2010–2019**, and **2010–2014 vs 2015–2019**. Transition y means level(y+1)/level(y), CPI August-to-August. Require complete window observations for a pair statistic, report all eligibility/missingness counts, use school-mean sector-centered growth, and report covariance/correlation and paired tuition/housing covariance. Label these descriptive across-school statistics; repeated identities, residency sectors and common economic years do not yield independent macro history. Compare all admissible versus source-quality subsets without tuning windows after results.
5. Validate with known-process fixtures: deterministic shared growth gives zero centered dispersion, persistent school effects survive disjoint windows, missing years are excluded explicitly, and a future membership change cannot alter origin eligibility. Verify dictionary mapping, unknown flags, fixed-case sensitivity and artifact/source identities. Add deterministic offline reproduction to CI, run repository checks, and document findings and the next concrete action.

## Guardrails and exit condition

This work may show persistent heterogeneity, annual noise, source artifacts or weak identification; it does not predetermine a replacement model. No correction/spread layer, probability remapping, financial default, saved-data schema, app version or candidate activation changes. A new candidate requires a separately committed protocol before its scoring and candidate-specific cost and funding evidence.

Complete when official dictionary coverage is explicit, fixed-case source-quality sensitivity and origin-cohort disjoint-window diagnostics are reproducible, meaningful fixtures pass, and progress/continuity notes distinguish findings from inference. If source retrieval fails, retain the specific failure and unclassified years rather than fabricating a mapping.

## Diagnostic amendment after initial results, before additional comparisons

The prespecified adjacent transition windows show mostly weak/negative school-mean growth correlations; their boundary price observation is shared. These are consumed descriptive results, not a promotion test. Preserve them. Before interpreting persistence, additionally calculate **2000–2004 vs 2006–2010** and **2000–2008 vs 2011–2019** transition windows, which share no price observation. Compare all quality-admissible complete pairs with a diagnostic subset excluding any school with an absolute annual real log change above **0.25** in either component in the union of the two windows. Report removed-school counts and original/gapped results separately; never delete source rows or original comparisons. This threshold is a fixed large-change screen, not a calibrated outlier rule, a causal test, or a source-correction license. No new candidate is specified or scored by this amendment.
