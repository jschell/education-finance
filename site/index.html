How the planner works

A funding goal, not a forecast

Success means every expense in your selected coverage goal is paid when due. A tuition-only result does not mean living expenses are funded.

Transparent uncertainty

Monte Carlo varies investment returns using a lognormal model with three saving stages. It does not predict aid, admissions, future prices, or every possible market crisis.

Keep a portable backup

Save explicitly to this browser and export regularly. Browser data can be cleared. There is no cross-device sync or account login.
