01 Student & college costs

Path labels organize scenarios. Enter costs yourself; selecting a label does not fill school prices. Budgets cover nine attendance months per year; include summer costs in your annual allowance if needed.

02 Savings & funding

Balance at the start of the valuation month. Use funds assigned only to this student. Restricted-account eligibility and taxes are not evaluated.

Optional contributions, aid & income

Grants are assumed renewable for the entered duration, paid at the two tuition dates and applied tuition first. Income is available in nine equal monthly amounts; unused income is not saved. Loans are not grants.

03 Growth & uncertainty

Illustrative starting values, not recommended investment profiles. Enter rates appropriate to your funds. Deterministic growth is a compound annual rate; Monte Carlo uses an arithmetic expected return and annual volatility. All returns are nominal, net of fees and any assumed tax drag.

Understand the result.

A funding goal, not a forecast

Success means every expense in your selected coverage goal is paid when due. A tuition-only result does not mean living expenses are funded.

Transparent uncertainty

Monte Carlo varies investment returns using a lognormal model with three saving stages. It does not predict aid, admissions, future prices, or every possible market crisis.
